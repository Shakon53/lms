# Student guide

## Start learning

1. Sign in and open **Home** to see current courses and programs.
2. Open a course and select the next available lesson.
3. Complete the lesson requirements. A lesson may require video completion, a
   quiz, an assignment, or a minimum reading time.
4. Use **Notes** for private revision notes and **Discussions** for course questions.

## Submit work

- Open assignments and quizzes from their lesson so the submission is connected
  to course progress.
- Check the submission status before leaving the page.
- If a deadline or access rule prevents submission, contact the instructor and
  include the course name, lesson, and your account email.

## Certificates and account help

- Certificates appear on the profile after the applicable completion and
  evaluation requirements are met.
- Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page if you cannot access the account.
- Never share a password or one-time sign-in link with another person.
